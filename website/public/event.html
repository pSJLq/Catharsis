Event rules

  • The ranking is by SkyBlock Level gained between the start and the end of the event. Ties go to more SkyBlock XP gained.
  • Prizes: 1st place 100 SOMI, 2nd place 50 SOMI, paid to the purse of the winning account after the event.
  • Abusing a bug or exploit, botting, macros or auto-clickers, and playing several accounts (multi-accounting) lead to disqualification.
  • Found a bug? Report it instead of using it. Useful reports are credited.
  • Unusual activity is reviewed by hand before prizes are paid; nobody is banned automatically. The standings are provisional until that review is done.
  • Staff and test accounts are not ranked.
  • This is a testnet event. Testnet tokens and items have no value.
